Frequently asked questions

How does Champ Client install?

You download two Windows files, champ.bat and champ.exe, and run them while Minecraft 1.21.11 is open. The bat only starts the exe; the exe signs in to your Champ account, checks the license, this PC and your network, and injects the menu into that running game. Nothing is placed in your mods/ folder, there is no jar to install, and you never have to restart Minecraft afterwards. The download button gives you the Windows pair or the Linux .tar.gz for the system you are on, and asks you to choose when it cannot tell.

How quickly will I receive my product after payment?

Thanks to instant delivery, the product is available in your account immediately after payment. You will also receive your license confirmation by email.

Does it work with Sodium and Iris shaders?

Yes. Champ Client is built to run alongside Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore and Iris shaders on Minecraft 1.21.11, and it renders inside the same frame as them.

Which payment methods are supported?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Credit cards, debit cards, iDEAL and country-specific local payment options are supported.
