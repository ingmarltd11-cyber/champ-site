Frequently asked questions

How do I install it?

Download the Windows pair or the Linux .tar.gz. On Windows, keep champ.bat and champ.exe in the same folder and run the bat while Minecraft 1.21.11 is open. The bat only starts the exe; the exe signs in, checks your license and this PC, and injects into the running game. Nothing goes into mods/, and you don't restart Minecraft. If the download can't tell which system you are on, it asks first.

How quickly do I get access after paying?

Straight away. Stripe confirms the payment, the license appears in your dashboard, and the same details go to your email. If the dashboard looks empty, reload it once; it reads the license on load.

How does the weekly plan work?

€2.99 buys 7 days of full access. It is a one-time payment, so it does not renew. When it ends, you can buy another week or move to monthly or lifetime. The same every-module access applies.

Does it work with Sodium and Iris?

Yes. It runs next to Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore and Iris on 1.21.11 and draws inside the same frame as the world, so your shaders stay on.

Which payment methods can I use?

Card, iDEAL and local payment methods are available through Stripe. The whole checkout runs on Stripe; we never see your card details.
