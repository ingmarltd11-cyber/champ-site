Is Champ Client an injection or a mod?

Champ Client is a 100% native Fabric Mod (.jar). You do not need any external injection tools or DLL injectors. You simply put the file into your Minecraft mods/ directory with Fabric Loader 0.16+ installed.

How quickly will I receive my product after payment?

Thanks to instant delivery, the product is available in your account immediately after payment. You will also receive your license confirmation by email.

Yes! Champ Client is optimized to work smoothly alongside Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, and Iris shaders on Minecraft 1.21.11.
